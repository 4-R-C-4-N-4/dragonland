# Decisions

Provisional calls made during the MVP build where the docs were silent or ambiguous. Each one overrides
`docs/implementation.md` until Ivy revises it. Code that depends on one carries a `// DESIGN-TBD: Dn` comment.

| Id | Decision | Why / where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Paladin and Dragon start with **identical** hp / mp / level / attack. The class+line asymmetry (Paladin→Dragon 1.05×, Dragon→Paladin 0.65×) is left in on purpose; Rally healing the Paladin in a duel (D3) may offset it. Measure with the headless runner before rebalancing. | Ivy: "maybe 1 and 2 will balance each other out. let's see." `data/generals.json` |
| D2 | **MP is a cooldown.** Firing a special needs a full meter, MP ≥ `mpCost`, and the special off cooldown. It spends `mpCost` MP and the full meter. After `special.cooldownS` the MP is restored to max. No other MP regen. | Ivy: "mp regen should be simply a cooldown, kiss". `SpecialDef.cooldownS` |
| D3 | In a duel, **Rally heals the Paladin himself** by `amount` (there are no troops left to heal, or they are not in the duel). Breath hits the opposing general. | Docs silent; Rally would otherwise be a no-op in duels. |
| D4 | Generals start each battle at full HP and MP. HP does not persist between battles. | MVP has no towns or healing. |
| D5 | General `level` is displayed but **not** used in combat math yet. Hook: `levelTerm()` in combat returns 0. | Formula in §2.4 has no level term. |
| D6 | Special `amount` is a **flat** value (heal or damage). It does not go through the class/line formula. | Simplest; placeholder specials. |
| D7 | Enemy troops are generated fresh each battle from AI loadout unit counts, with seeded names. Enemy casualties are shown on the result screen but not persisted. | AI loadouts cannot reference the player roster. |
| D8 | The builder requires every company troop to be in a squad (no auto-grouping). A troop in the roster but not in any squad is simply not in the company. | §2.6 "Simplest" option. |
| D9 | **All armies are blended (Ivy).** Mono-class mirror loadouts were removed. Test-only loadouts (`"test": true`, never picked by the enemy commit): `lean-human` (60% Human / 40% Beast) vs `lean-beast` (mirror image, same structure and general), and `even` (50/50) for side-bias checks. AI loadouts range from 30% to 70% Human and are normalized to about 50% stage-2 troops, so the matrix measures composition, disposition and formation rather than stage-2 count. | `data/loadouts-ai.json` |
| D10 | Player is side 0 (left, faces +x); enemy is side 1 (right, faces −x). | Convention. |
| D11 | During the duel, surviving troops (on timeout) freeze and take no part; they survive to the roster. | §2.10: "generals duel". |
| D12 | Stage-3 units (`paladin`, `dragon`) live in `units.json` for general stats (range, cooldown, speed, size) but can never be roster troops. | §2.3 |
| D13 | **Duel tie-break** when both generals hit 0 HP on the same blow (or tie on HP fraction at `DUEL_MAX_ROUNDS`): winner = `seed % 2 === 0 ? 0 : 1`. A fixed side (e.g. always side 0) silently biased every symmetric/evenly-matched battle toward side 0 across a seed ensemble — confirmed by `mirror-human` vs itself landing at ~87/13 before this fix, ~50/50 after. Still deterministic per seed, so replay is exact. | `src/sim/duel.ts` |
| D14 | Each troop's deploy position gets a small seeded jitter (`TROOP_SPACING * JITTER_FRAC`, new constant) around its formation slot. With zero other randomness in combat, identical squads always reached the exact same first-contact geometry, so an ensemble of seeds for the same matchup produced one deterministic outcome repeated — no win-rate curve to tune. Jitter gives genuinely different (but still seed-deterministic, replay-exact) initial contacts. | `src/sim/world.ts`, `JITTER_FRAC` |
| D15 | **Duel-entry chip damage.** On a timeout-triggered duel (not a double-wipe), each general takes `DUEL_ENTRY_CHIP_PER_SURVIVOR * (enemy's surviving troop count)` flat damage before the duel's blow exchange starts. Rationale: generals rarely take any damage during the 180s fight (troops fight each other, not the general, until their own company is dead), so a side that dominated the troop battle but didn't fully wipe the enemy before the clock ran out would otherwise enter a symmetric-general duel as a near coin flip — erasing the class/tuning signal the acceptance test depends on. This is a deliberate deviation from the literal "duel uses only general stats" reading of §2.10, documented as the simplest fix; `// DESIGN-TBD: D15` marks the code. | `src/sim/skirmish.ts`, `DUEL_ENTRY_CHIP_PER_SURVIVOR` |
| D16 | Troop/general processing order alternates by tick parity (`world.tick % 2`) so neither side gets a persistent first-mover advantage in simultaneous attack/movement resolution within a tick. Still a pure function of `tick`, so determinism and replay are unaffected. | `src/sim/behavior.ts` |
| D17 | **Bodies may not overlap.** The M1 spatial grid was never populated, so steering separation was a no-op and whole squads collapsed onto one point. Fixed: grid rebuilt every tick, plus a hard body-overlap pass (`behaviors.overlapIterations`) using `bodyRadius`; generals are immovable anchors. Mixed squads now steer to centroid + their deploy offset (`behaviors.mixed.formationPull`) so they hold formation. | `src/sim/behavior.ts` |
| D18 | Attack cooldowns reverted from the M1 tuner's halved values (~0.5 s) back to the starting values: the main melee was over in ~15 s, too fast for pause-and-plan. Archer/Ranger cooldown 2.4/2.3 s and Raven/Hawk 0.9/0.85 s so the class-neutral mirror (`--set CLASS_MOD=0`) lands near Human 90% / Beast 5% instead of 100/0. Unit stats are still placeholders. | `data/units.json` |
| D19 | **Open: class lean is close to decisive in AI-vs-AI.** With blended armies: a 60/40 class lean against its mirror image wins 99% either way round (100 runs each); `even` vs `even` is 42/58 (no meaningful side bias). Across the five AI loadouts, composition, disposition and formation outweigh class lean: Mixed Host (50/50) beats Human Legion (70% Human) 100%, and Human Legion vs Human Skirmishers is 55%. `CLASS_MOD` stays 0.25. | `npm run sim -- --a lean-human --b lean-beast`, `--matrix` |
| D20 | **Demo auto-recruit (Ivy: "get rid of whatever troop pool thing... for the demo testing and mock that logic").** With `ROSTER_AUTO_RECRUIT` = 1 (the default), whenever a squad composition needs more of a unit than the roster has, the builder recruits fresh named troops (`store.recruit`, `recruitTroops`). Deaths still remove the fallen troops by name, and replacements are new recruits. Set it to 0 in `data/constants.json` or the debug constants panel to bring back the scarce roster. | `src/ui/builder/index.ts` `fill()`, `src/persist/roster.ts` |
| D21 | **Dispositions reworked (Ivy: "fierce goes after the general, mixed fights other troops, guard protects the general").** Fierce: Advance targets the enemy general, fights anything already in reach, and flanks along a field edge while more than `flankDist` (250) away. Mixed: each troop targets the nearest enemy troop from its own position at its own speed (never the general while troops remain). Guard: threats within 160 of its general's current position first; commands apply (Advance fights forward like Mixed, Hold/Fall Back stay with the general). Measured (`tools/dispo.ts`, mirror armies, 20 battles/cell): all-Guard beats all-Fierce 95%, all-Mixed beats all-Guard 100%, all-Fierce vs all-Mixed 50%; overall Mixed 78%, Guard 38%, Fierce 34%. Without flanking, Fierce lost ~98% (divers never reached the general). Mixed is still the strongest generalist. Run auto-arrange default: Mixed/Mixed/Guard (40% of 30 campaigns won). All knobs in `data/behaviors.json`. | `src/sim/behavior.ts`, `tools/dispo.ts`, `tools/runsim.ts` |
| D23 | **Enemy dispositions follow a seeded doctrine, shown on the encounter card (Ivy).** Balanced (1 Guard, +1 Fierce with 4 squads), Assault (Fierce-heavy), Defensive (half Guard), Skirmish (all Mixed). Fierce goes to the fastest squads (they flank), Guard to the toughest (Z line, higher stage). `tools/runsim.ts` now also runs the browser's in-battle enemy AI (it previously didn't, which overstated player win rates). Campaign bot, default Mixed/Mixed/Guard, 30 runs: doctrine alone 40% -> 10% won; plus in-battle AI 0%. `enemyTroopsPerDepth` 8 -> 6 brings it to 37% won (6.8 avg victories); all-Fierce 3%, all-Mixed 40%. Very sensitive: recovery 0.7 gives 87%. | `src/run/engine.ts`, `data/run.json` |
| D24 | **Heroes and specials (Ivy: specials should hit the whole field like Dragon Force; every max-level sprite can be a hero).** All 15 stage-3 species are generals, each with one data-defined special made of effects: damage over the whole field, a wave across the field through the enemy general, N random strikes, a direct hit on the general, army-wide heal, or timed side-wide buff/debuff (damage, speed, defense, damage over time). Class and line modifiers apply to special damage. Mastery upgrades scale `special.power`. Every special names an fx art id (`assets/fx/<fx>`); placeholder visuals until that art exists. Starting troops per hero class (Ivy): Human+Mage, Mage+Construct, Construct+Beast, Beast+Ethereal, Ethereal+Human (each partner is neutral to the hero class). The other three classes (the other neutral, quarry, nemesis) unlock by defeating an enemy general of that class. | `data/generals.json`, `src/sim/specials.ts` |
| D22 | **Wounded recovery (Ivy).** After a victory, a seeded 50% (`woundedRecoveryOnWin`) of the fallen rejoin as wounded survivors (they also get the victory XP); none after a defeat. | `src/run/engine.ts`, `data/run.json` |
